Places will always be a part of us, and our stories in some intangible way. I create simple, thoughtful artwork that gives you a sense of place and maybe an awareness of the places you've been. Whether it's a tiny house made from reclaimed wood or a lonely painting of the foothills rendered on reused canvas. My hope is that these pieces will serve as a physical remembrance of time spent & a little piece of where you've been that you can take with you wherever you may go.
