Places will always be a part of us, and our stories in some intangible way.

I create simple, thoughtful artwork that gives you a sense of place and an awareness of the places you've been.

Whether it's a nostalgic object made from reclaimed wood or a lonely painting of the open sea rendered on linen canvas. My hope is that these pieces will serve as a physical remembrance of time spent & a little piece of where you've been that you can take with you where ever you may go.
